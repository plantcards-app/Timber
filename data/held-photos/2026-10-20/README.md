  of 'JS2' × 'Red Prince', with no species. The label's *florida* is kept and the code taken from the patent. A US
  nursery sells 'JS4' as Watermelon Kisses ([Inference] the same plant). Part of the ALL SUMMER series, like the
  held ALL SUMMER RED.

- **Silver Scrolls** is credited to Charles Oliver (Primrose Path), not Terra Nova.
- Weigela toxicity: no ASPCA or RHS listing found; "No known hazard" is the absence of a listing.
